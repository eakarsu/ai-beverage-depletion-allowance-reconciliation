# Beverage Depletion Allowance Reconciliation

Reconcile distributor depletion, billback, and promotional allowances to verified account-level sales.

**Primary buyer:** Beverage producers and distributors. **Evidence:** distribution agreements, products, territories, depletion reports, accounts, volume tiers, price promotions, billbacks, allowances, claims, and credits.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Distributor agreement library
- Brand SKU registry
- Territory account mapping
- Depletion report ingestion
- Account eligibility validation
- Volume tier calculation
- Promotional period control
- Billback calculation
- Display allowance evidence
- Duplicate claim detection
- Distributor deduction matching
- Claim approval workflow
- Credit memo reconciliation
- Accrual true-up
- Brand distributor analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4708>. API: `5708`.
